Lesson 7 · 14 min

Rules that are coming

An agent manager doesn't have to become a lawyer, but needs to know what already applies, what has a deadline, what is under discussion and who owns each item. This lesson is short on purpose: it builds your area's compliance timeline and shows that the program's pieces already cover much of the evidence a regulation or a client will ask for.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1What already applies and what has a set date
  2. 2How to handle what was only reported
  3. 3What reaches you through the customer before it reaches you through the law
  4. 4Which pieces of the program already serve as evidence

Goal: build the compliance timeline for your area: what already applies, what has a date, what is under discussion, and who looks after each item.

02 · Three precautions and one advantage

The details of the rules are in Profissional 2030 Base; here is what the manager does with them.

1. Fact, date and source

Each item on the timeline has its source and the date of the source. Anything that only showed up in a blog or in an article citing another article goes in as "reported, check it", with the date of the news.

A timeline ages fast: the Digital Omnibus changed EU AI Act dates in 2026. Set the next review, every three months.

At Horizonte"PL 2338/2023: reported as stalled in the Chamber (article of 08/24/2026, secondary source). Owner: Carlos with the accounting firm. Review in January 2027."

2. Who it applies to

The 02/12/2027 date applies to anyone who sells or uses AI in the European market. Horizonte sells in the interior of São Paulo: today, it doesn't apply. Write that on the timeline, with the reason, instead of deleting the item.

If the company ever sells abroad, the item changes columns. That's why it stays on record.

AI literacyAt Horizonte it isn't a legal obligation, but it's cheap good practice: training the team (the people course in the program) is the literacy.

3. When to call legal

Call whoever handles legal matters when: the agent decides something about people (credit, hiring), data leaves the country, a customer asks for a written statement, or you're unsure whether a rule applies to you.

Your role is to have the timeline, the owners and the evidence organized. The legal opinion belongs to someone else.

At HorizonteThe support summary sends customer conversations to a foreign service without a contract (F10). That goes to legal and to the LGPD lesson in Professional 2030 Base.

4. The advantage of having done the program

When a customer or a rule asks "show me how you control AI," the answer is pieces you already have: the responsibility matrix, the identity and log checklist, the evaluation set, the dashboard and the version history.

Put them in one folder with an index. The next compliance question becomes a lookup in the folder, not a project.

04 · Practice

Horizonte's timeline is for practice; your area's is for real.

1 · Horizonte's timeline

20 min

Without looking at the tab, build the timeline for Horizonte's sales area using the template. Use the four columns, the lab failures that touch personal data, and what a large customer might ask for. Run the review prompt.

Done when: the timeline has at least 6 items across the 4 columns; each has a source with a date, "does it apply to us" with a reason, an owner and a next step; PL 2338 is marked "reported, check it"; and the next review date is written down.

2 · Your area's timeline

40 min

Build your area's timeline and the evidence folder index with the pieces you already have from this program. Take the questions the prompt flagged to whoever handles legal matters.

Done when: the timeline is saved with owners and a next review; the folder index points to at least 5 pieces that already exist; and the questions to legal were sent, with a date.

Rule details: Rules with set dates, in Professional 2030 Base.
