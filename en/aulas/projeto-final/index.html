Lesson 8 · 20 min

Final project in Gestoria + What changed

The final project of the program is to manage an operation from end to end: understand it, redesign it, divide the work, equip the agent, run it, evaluate it and improve it. You log the operation in Gestoria, build the 14-step report and run it through the rubric that brings together the program's courses. At the end, you'll see what changed in the market, with a date and a source.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  3. 3The 8-item rubric and the 7 required items
  4. 4What changed through October 2026 in governance, rules and certifications

Goal: run an operation from end to end through the 14 steps in Gestoria and get the final report approved by the program's 8-item rubric.

02 · From Gestoria to the signed report

Gestoria is a local lab: it stores process, roles, runs, decisions, evaluations and improvements, and exports everything as JSON. It does not generate a report or run a real model. The report is this lesson's template.

1. Register the process and the agent

Open Gestoria and, in Processes, create the process with a human owner, expected outcome, weekly volume, current human time per case, cost per hour, accuracy target, lab budget and the steps of the new version.

In Workforce, create the agent's role: owner, planned model, mission, receives, delivers, can, cannot, context, Skills, tools and initial autonomy (at most "Prepares").

At HorizonteProcess "Order via WhatsApp → invoicing", owner Patrícia, 170 cases per week, 4.4 min of human time per case, R$ 50 per hour (average of the human hours from G4: R$ 2,502 ÷ 49.8 h), accuracy target 95%, 8 steps. Agent Hora, owner Patrícia, initial autonomy "Prepares".

2. Run and observe

In Overview, simulate at least one case of each scenario: routine within the policy, conflicting information and a request outside the policy. In Human decisions, approve or send back each one, with a justification that another person can understand.

Each simulation costs R$ 0.38 and 42 teaching seconds, with no charge. Record in the report the 5-indicator dashboard from lesson 4 with the numbers from your real case or from Horizonte.

Good justification"Sent back: the customer asked for 15% and the agent's authority limit is 5%; prepare a proposal up to 10% for Patrícia to approve."

3. Evaluate and improve

In Evaluations, log at least 10 of your 30 cases, with answer key, outcome and class, including a critical one, if there is one. To promote an agent level, the tool asks for 100 cases of the current version, with no critical errors: the project does not require this.

In Continuous improvement, log the LOOP-R cycle and advance from Proposed to Promoted, one stage at a time, with the evidence. The tool has no "rolled back" stage: the rollback goes in the report, and the agent can be paused.

At HorizonteImprovement "route table in the context": Promoted with the evidence from cycle 0001; in the report, the week 8 rollback and the proposed v2.3.

4. Report, rubric and signature

Export the JSON in Lab data, fill in the 14-step report with the pieces from the courses and run it through the rubric: 75 points or more and all 7 required items. Run the review panel prompt before handing it in.

Whoever built it does not sign it. At Horizonte, Patrícia builds it, Rafael confirms the safeguards and Carlos signs it, and only then does he decide whether Hora goes into billing.

Honest limitGestoria stores the data in this browser. Export the JSON and keep a copy: clearing the browser erases the work.

04 · What changed · October 2026

Quarterly panel, swapped out at each course revision. Each item with a date, source and type of claim. The method in lessons 1 to 7 does not depend on any product mentioned.

1. Governance behind adoption

Okta, AI Agents at Work 2026 (survey by an identity vendor): almost two thirds of organizations apply weaker security controls to agents than to employees. Deloitte (09/17/2026): 50% of Brazilian companies have adopted AI and 27% have mature governance.

Gartner (02/17/2026, forecast): spending on AI governance goes from US$ 492 million in 2026 to more than US$ 1 billion in 2030.

For the projectThe distance between using and governing is the space of the AI manager: matrix, checklist, evaluation, dashboard and a cycle that loops back.

2. Incidents and rules

Stanford AI Index (04/13/2026): documented AI incidents went from 233 to 362. EU AI Act: the Digital Omnibus package (07/24/2026) postponed the Annex III high-risk rules to 12/02/2027; AI literacy already applies in the EU.

Brazil: Bill PL 2338/2023 was reported as stalled in the Chamber of Deputies (secondary source, 08/24/2026; check the official record).

For the projectUpdate the lesson 7 timeline at each revision. A date that changes does not change the method.

3. Certifications that exist

Governance: AIGP (IAPP, since 04/2024), strong on privacy and legal. Security: AAISM (ISACA, 08/2025), requires CISM or CISSP. Management system: ISO/IEC 42001, which certifies the company; the auditor or implementer course is for the individual.

In the October 2026 search, no independent certification for managing agents was found. The vendor ones (NVIDIA, GitHub, Google, Oracle) are about building agents, not managing them.

ReadingAnyone who needs AIGP or ISO/IEC 42001 for the job still needs them. The INEMA certification is a qualification.

4. How to read these reports

Who pays and what do they sell? Okta and Microsoft sell the solution to the problem they measure; consultancies sell the transformation they describe. How did they measure? A survey with a sample, self-reported experience or a forecast?

An analysis on The Microsoft Cloud blog points out that the Work Trend Index 2026 numbers should not carry a business case on their own. A report is a map, never proof of your case: the proof is your dashboard.
